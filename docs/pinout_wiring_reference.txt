--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
POWER ARCHITECTURE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Battery input:  2x Ingco 20V 4Ah Li-Ion in parallel = 20V nominal, 8Ah
                (built-in BMS, no external BMS required)

RAIL 1 - 12V Motor Rail
  Source:  BUCK-300W-20A adjustable buck, input from battery, output set to 12V
  Fuse:    20A blade fuse between battery and buck input
  Feeds:   BTS7960 Left (B+ and B-)
           BTS7960 Right (B+ and B-)
           BTS7960 Sweeper (B+ and B-)
  Decoupling: 470uF 25V electrolytic across VCC and GND on each BTS7960 module

RAIL 2 - 5V Pi Rail
  Source:  Waveshare UPS HAT (B) mounted on Pi 5 GPIO header
           Powered by 2x Samsung 30Q 18650 cells in the HAT
           Charged via 8.4V 2A charger into HAT charging port
  Feeds:   Raspberry Pi 5 directly via GPIO header (no USB-C involved)
  Note:    Pi is completely independent of the Ingco battery system

RAIL 3 - 5V ESP32 Rail
  Source:  Fixed 5V 10A buck (WG-1224-S0510), input from battery, output 5V
  Fuse:    5A blade fuse between battery and buck input
  Feeds:   ESP32 VIN pin
           GREEN LED via 330 ohm resistor
           YELLOW LED via 330 ohm resistor
           RED LED via 330 ohm resistor

Star ground: all rail GND, battery negative, ESP32 GND, and Pi GND
  tied together at a single star ground point.
  Pi GND also wired directly to ESP32 GND as UART common reference.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BTS7960 H-BRIDGE WIRING (all 3 modules, identical connections)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

BTS7960 Pin | Connects To                     | Notes
------------|---------------------------------|-------------------------------
R_PWM       | ESP32 RPWM pin for this motor   | Forward speed, duty 0-255
L_PWM       | ESP32 LPWM pin for this motor   | Reverse speed, duty 0-255
R_EN        | Module VCC (tied HIGH)          | Hardwired, no ESP32 pin needed
L_EN        | Module VCC (tied HIGH)          | Hardwired, no ESP32 pin needed
VCC         | 5V from Rail 3                  | Logic supply
GND         | Star ground point               | Common ground
B+          | 12V Motor Rail (Rail 1)         | Motor power supply positive
B-          | Star ground point               | Motor power supply negative
M+          | Motor terminal A                | Either motor wire
M-          | Motor terminal B                | Other motor wire
R_IS        | Not connected (leave floating)  | Current sense, not used
L_IS        | Not connected (leave floating)  | Current sense, not used

MODULE ASSIGNMENTS:
  BTS7960 Left:    GPIO4 (R_PWM), GPIO25 (L_PWM) -> Left drive motor
  BTS7960 Right:   GPIO27 (R_PWM), GPIO26 (L_PWM) -> Right drive motor
  BTS7960 Sweeper: GPIO19 (R_PWM), GPIO23 (L_PWM) -> Sweeper brush motor


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STATUS LEDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each LED wired in series with a 330 ohm resistor between the ESP32 GPIO
pin and GND. ESP32 GPIO drives HIGH to illuminate, LOW to extinguish.

Colour  | GPIO | Meaning
--------|------|----------------------------------
Green   | 21   | Manual mode active
Yellow  | 22   | Autonomous mode active
Red     | 33   | Fault or e-stop active (boot default)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UART WIRING (ESP32 <-> Raspberry Pi)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Use Dupont jumper wires. Keep under 300mm. No level shifter needed,
both devices operate at 3.3V logic.

ESP32 GPIO16 (RX2) <- Pi physical pin 8  (GPIO14, UART0 TX)
ESP32 GPIO17 (TX2) -> Pi physical pin 10 (GPIO15, UART0 RX)
ESP32 GND          -- Pi physical pin 6  (GND)

Packet: 8 bytes at 115200 baud, 10Hz, big-endian
  Bytes 0-1: bearing_error  (int16, milliradians)
  Bytes 2-5: ground_distance (float32, metres)
  Byte  6:   mode_flag      (uint8, 0=no target, 1=target detected)
  Byte  7:   checksum       (uint8, XOR of bytes 0-6)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CAMERA MODULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Module:         Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3 Wide
Connection:     CSI ribbon cable to Pi 5 CSI port
Mounting:       220mm above ground, 25 degrees depression below horizontal
                10mm rearward offset from robot front face
                Fixed mount, mechanically locked, no adjustable angle
Focus:          Fixed at 0.8m (AfMode=0, LensPosition=1.25 dioptres)
                Autofocus disabled to prevent blur during robot motion
Stream:         MJPEG on port 8080
                Home WiFi: http://192.168.0.29:8080/stream
                Pi hotspot: http://10.42.0.1:8080/stream


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WIRE GAUGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

18AWG:  Battery to master switch, battery to buck converters,
        buck converter outputs to BTS7960 B+ terminals,
        BTS7960 M+ and M- to motors
24AWG:  BTS7960 VCC and GND logic supply lines,
        Buck converter GND to star ground
Dupont: All signal wires (PWM, UART, LED signals)
        Never carry motor current through Dupont wires


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DONT USE / WARNINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Never drive R_PWM and L_PWM on the same BTS7960 simultaneously.
  This causes shoot-through and will destroy the module.
  Firmware enforces exclusive activation via driveMotor().

Never power the Pi via USB-C and the UPS HAT simultaneously.
  Use only one power source at a time.

Never switch the UPS HAT power switch off while the Pi is running.
  Always run: sudo shutdown now, wait for LED to stop, then switch off.

Always turn the UPS HAT switch to OFF before inserting or removing
  the 18650 batteries.
