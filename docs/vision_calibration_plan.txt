SUMMARY: Distance readings from the vision pipeline are currently indicative
only. Bearing (left/right angle) is reliable now. Distance accuracy requires
physical calibration once the robot is assembled.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PLACEHOLDERS REQUIRING CALIBRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. FOCAL_LENGTH_PX = 259.1                              CRITICAL
   What it is: estimated from Camera Module 3 Wide spec sheet, not measured.
   Effect: every distance reading scales linearly with this value.
           A 10% error here means every distance is wrong by 10%.
   Fix: OpenCV chessboard calibration (see sequence below).
   Where in code: harvest_vision.py, line near top of configuration section.

2. CAMERA_HEIGHT_MM = 220.0                             MEDIUM
   What it is: design value from CAD, not physically measured.
   Effect: affects all ground distance calculations, especially at close range.
   Fix: measure actual lens centre height above ground with tape measure
        after camera bracket is mounted and bolted to chassis.
   Where in code: harvest_vision.py, configuration section.

3. DEPRESSION_DEG = 25.0                                MEDIUM
   What it is: design angle from specification, not physically verified.
   Effect: affects ground distance projection geometry.
   Fix: measure actual angle with a digital level or protractor once the
        3D printed camera mount is installed. Manufacturing tolerances can
        introduce 1-2 degrees of error.
   Where in code: harvest_vision.py, configuration section.

4. CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD = 0.5                           FIELD TUNE
   What it is: minimum YOLO detection confidence to count as a valid ball.
   Effect: too high causes missed detections, too low causes false positives.
   Fix: test in actual outdoor lighting conditions on real macadamia nuts.
        Lower to 0.4 if too many misses. Raise to 0.6 if false triggers occur.
   Where in code: harvest_vision.py, configuration section.

5. CAP_DIST = 0.15m  (in ESP32 firmware: CAP_DIST = 0.15)  FIELD TUNE
   What it is: estimated distance at which ball disappears below camera FOV.
   Effect: triggers the Zone 3 blind capture drive too early or too late.
   Fix: place a ball in front of the mounted camera and physically move it
        toward the robot until it disappears from the bottom of the stream.
        Measure that distance from the robot front face. Use it as CAP_DIST.
   Where in code: harvest_vision.py and main_controller.ino.

6. CAMERA_OFFSET_MM = 10.0                              LOW PRIORITY
   What it is: rearward offset of lens from robot front face.
   Effect: currently not used in any active calculation. Documented only.
   Fix: no immediate action needed.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CALIBRATION SEQUENCE (DO IN THIS ORDER AFTER ROBOT IS ASSEMBLED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Physical measurements
   - Mount camera bracket, tighten all bolts
   - Measure actual lens centre height above flat ground (target: 220mm)
   - Measure actual depression angle with digital level (target: 25 degrees)
   - Update CAMERA_HEIGHT_MM and DEPRESSION_DEG in harvest_vision.py

Step 2: OpenCV chessboard calibration (replaces FOCAL_LENGTH_PX)
   - Print a standard OpenCV chessboard (9x6 inner corners, 25mm squares)
   - With camera mounted at final position, photograph the board from at
     least 20 different angles and distances
   - Run OpenCV calibrateCamera() to obtain the 3x3 camera matrix
   - Extract fx (top-left element of matrix, units: pixels)
   - Update FOCAL_LENGTH_PX = fx in harvest_vision.py
   - This is approximately a half-day task

Step 3: Distance validation
   - Place a golf ball at exactly 500mm from the robot front face
   - Check pipeline distance reading vs tape measure
   - Repeat at 1000mm and 1500mm
   - All readings should agree within 50mm at these distances
   - If not, recheck CAMERA_HEIGHT_MM and DEPRESSION_DEG measurements

Step 4: Capture zone threshold
   - With camera running, place ball directly in front of robot
   - Slowly move ball toward robot until it disappears from stream bottom edge
   - Measure distance from robot front face at that point
   - Update CAP_DIST in harvest_vision.py and CAP_DIST in main_controller.ino

Step 5: Confidence threshold
   - Test in actual outdoor lighting on real macadamia nuts
   - Observe detection rate and false positive rate
   - Adjust CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD up or down as needed


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS RELIABLE NOW (NO CALIBRATION NEEDED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Bearing error (left/right angle in milliradians): reliable now.
  The bearing calculation uses only pixel_x offset from frame centre divided
  by focal length. Small focal length errors affect this far less than distance.

- Detection itself (is a ball visible or not): reliable now.
  YOLO confidence and class prediction do not depend on geometric calibration.

- UART communication and ESP32 steering response: working and validated.

- Zone transitions and state machine logic: working and validated.
